# user story 1
John is a visitor who is new to hiking in Seattle. He wants to find hiking trails that match his interests and discover unique wildlife in Seattle. He wants an easy-to-use website with an interactive map, clear trail information, and wildlife descriptions. He wants to explore local nature without needing specialized knowledge. He needs simple navigation and accessible information.
# user story 2
Tim is a hiking enthusiast. He enjoys hiking in Seattle and surrounding areas. He wants to explore different hiking trails around Seattle and learns about the wildlife found along each trail. So he starts to use this tool which contains a map showing hiking routes and the distribution of wildlife species. He also wants to filter trails by location, difficulty, and wildlife species to help him choose a hiking destination. He does have basic hiking experience but limited knowledge of local wildlife or mapping tools.
